WebGL 2 Required

      

        UFO COMBAT needs a browser with WebGL 2 support (any recent version of
        Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Please update your browser, or enable
        hardware acceleration in its settings, and reload the page.
      

    

  


  

    

      
The graphics card stopped responding

      

        The browser lost its connection to the graphics card (the WebGL
        context was lost): drawing a frame took too long, the computer went to
        sleep, or the graphics driver restarted. Your world and all your
        settings are saved and stay as they are. Reload to go on playing; if
        this keeps happening, reload once with lower graphics
        (High,
        without your individual graphics options) for that session only.
      

      

        
        
      

    

  


  

    

      
Keyboard & Mouse Required

      

        UFO COMBAT is a first-person 3D game that needs a physical keyboard and mouse
        for movement and looking around. Please open this page on a desktop or laptop
        computer to play.
